`task_object`: a proposed streaming architecture change

`user_request`: Our video-transcoding pipeline has three stages: ingest, transcode, publish. During traffic spikes, ingest accepts everything and buffers to disk (which fills), transcode falls behind, and publish starves. Two proposals on the table: (A) make ingest reject work when disk is 80% full; (B) propagate 'I can take N more items' signals from transcode back to ingest and let each stage bound its own queue. Choose or combine, with reasons.

  `product_intent`: "User uploads must not be silently lost; a clear 'try later' response is acceptable; transcoding lag is tolerable up to 30 min."
  `code`: "Current: ingest stage has unbounded disk buffer; transcode pool fixed; publish waits on transcode output queue (also unbounded)."
  `runtime`: "Last spike: disk 94% (near overflow), transcode lag 2h, publish idle 40% of the time (starved between batches)."
`constraints`:
  - media pipeline, 3 stages, bursty upload traffic
  - the two proposals come from two senior engineers who disagree
