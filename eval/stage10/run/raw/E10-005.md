## Decision

**Reject the PR as written.** The delta is a breaking change to a public, independently consumed contract, and it contradicts the accepted product stability commitment.

The rename is not merely internal: `order_id`/`event_type` are the wire-format field names that ~1,400 external integrators parse. Removing them under an internal style-guide lint rule transfers the breakage to every uncoordinated consumer, with no compatibility direction and no deprecation path. Style-guide consistency is explicitly "internal," so it is not a higher-priority security/correctness override and not a governed migration — the two GOOD CASE exceptions for MP-007/FP-008 do not apply.

The minimum correction is local to the delta: do not rename the public wire schema. Keep snake_case on the wire (exempt the public payload DTO from the style lint rule), and apply camelCase only to internal surfaces. If the team truly wants to migrate the public schema, that is a separate governed migration: keep emitting both field names for a deprecation window and give the ToS-mandated 6-month notice before removing the old names.
