## Verdict

**The team is right — as stated, the consultant's BLOCKER is invalid.** But the strength of your position comes from your *targeted health-check channels*, not from the proof alone. Four independent grounds:

1. **AX-001 (property-free finding).** "Every aerospace system needs observability" names no required property of *this* system. Pattern-universality is explicitly not an answer form. AX-003 does not rescue it either (D-08: contextual only): no applicable regulatory/certification non-negotiable was cited — see the one open item below.
2. **GC-008 applies (qualifier, not title).** seL4-class proof under an explicit, satisfied assumption set *discharges the questions actually proved* — scheduling/IPC functional correctness. "Missing service-style telemetry" is the registered naive false-positive for exactly this shape (MP-006; D-11 question-relative; no "more proof → less observability" scalar law; no telemetry-stack mandate).
3. **FP-007 precondition unmet.** Missing Distinguishing Evidence requires a *concrete unanswered operational question* not discharged by a stronger guarantee. The consultant named none. "No observability architecture" as a category label is not a question.
4. **The factual premise is contradicted by CODE.** You have targeted health-check channels for the outside-assumption-set classes that actually matter (sensor hardware faults, integration behavior). So "no observability architecture" is false as stated — what you lack is *fleet telemetry*, which MP-006 does not require.

**Burden shifts to the consultant**, with two named paths that could rehabilitate the demand (Q-037/Q-039 discipline):

- **Path A — name the question:** one concrete operational question *outside* the proof scope that current health-check channels cannot distinguish or derive (Q-037). If one exists, FP-007 becomes live and T-014 question-relative placement applies — not necessarily fleet telemetry.
- **Path B — cite the non-negotiable:** an applicable certification/recording requirement (AX-003). If cited and current channels don't satisfy it, the finding becomes legitimate on compliance grounds.

One caution for your side: do **not** argue "the proof means no observability needed." GC-008's qualifier explicitly keeps hardware/integration/ops questions outside the assumption set in play — you're compliant there because of the channels, not the proof. And note the asymmetry of cost: your verified build config is part of the assumption set, so instrumentation code is not free — it perturbs the verification perimeter and can force re-verification. Under AX-004, adding it without a property that needs it is the wrong direction.
