# PR #77 Review — auth-client retry

**mode: CHANGE_REVIEW · dp: DP-003 · overlays: overload (retry family) + integration (contract/rollout)** — explicit mode, so no AUTO routing basis. Object is a concrete delta to a running shared library; production is not yet failing (DP-005 not triggered).

## Verdict: do not merge as-is. The delta is a textbook FP-011 (Retry Amplification) generator shipped fleet-wide.

**Mechanism (S3–S4).** Q-028 asks: is retry here the bound, the amplifier, or both? **Both.** Per-call the count is bounded (3), so this is *not* an MP-003 unbounded-count defect (D-04 separation matters — "no retry bound" would be the wrong diagnosis). But as overload policy (MP-009/FP-011) it fails every qualifier:

- **Retries on 429.** The 4% of peak responses that are 429 are the auth path's existing rejection boundary doing its job. Retrying through them multiplies offered load ×4 on exactly the requests that signal saturation — 4% → ~16% of volume, landing on a service at 62% peak CPU. The boundary exists; the delta routes around its signal.
- **No jitter, fixed 100ms, 40 consumers.** Synchronized retry waves (FP-011's named observable signature).
- **No retry budget.** Recovery traffic and brownout periods get hammered; nothing stops retrying when the dependency is failing.
- **Retry ≠ idempotency (T-015).** POST /token has side effects on rate counters; up to 4 token requests per logical auth burns the very quota the rate limiter defends. Retrying 401/403 additionally wastes quota and +200ms on deterministic failures that cannot heal.

**GC check (S4).** GC-015 qualifier — "bounded, **jittered** retry **behind an effective path boundary**" — fails on jitter and on boundary-respect; bounded-count alone does not rescue it. GC-011 doesn't apply: these are independent consumers with a shared auto-bumping artifact, not coordinated internals.

**Rollout (Q-040/Q-041).** This is also an ungoverned behavioral contract change: 40 independent consumers inherit new latency (+200ms worst case) and volume (×4 on non-2xx) semantics via a *minor* auto-bump, with no opt-in, no compatibility direction (T-012), and the counterparty team (auth service capacity owner) unaware. Spread itself is a review signal, not a violation (GC-007) — the defect is *what* spreads, plus the missing governance of the spread.
