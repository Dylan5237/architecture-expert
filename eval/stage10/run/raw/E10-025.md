## Decision: No — do not modularize now

**Direct answer to "should we modularize now?"**: No. Defer explicit boundary placement until (a) a variation axis becomes identifiable from repeated same-axis edits, or (b) the idea is validated and the team commits — at which point boundary design is forward-synthesis work (DP-001), informed by the change history the prototype will have accumulated by then.

### Rationale

**Mechanism check (S3–S4).** The consultancy's finding maps to candidate FP-002 (Hidden Variation-Axis Leak). FP-002's precondition is a variation axis that *could have been identified in advance*. The pivot history shows the opposite: five whole-concept pivots (auth-idea → … → interactive docs), each rewriting >60% of the file. That is the signature of *the decision itself changing* during exploration, not of a stable decision leaking through a missing boundary. GC-003 (exploration with unknown variation axes) matches on its qualifier, not just its title: MP-002's Applies-When requires an identifiable change axis, and none is evidenced. GC-007 additionally covers the >60% rewrite spread — change spread is a review signal, not a violation. Constraints confirm this is still exploration: direction "still being discovered", 2 engineers, 4 more requested weeks of iteration.

**Why the consultancy claim fails (S1).** "Unacceptably monolithic, needs immediate modularization into clean layers" names no required property, no mechanism, no failure mode (AX-001: no property-free findings). Layering itself is a structure preference; per AX-004 the target is minimal complexity that protects the required property — here, validation learning throughput — which the current form is serving. Modularizing *now* would spend the 4 weeks of iteration capacity on speculative boundaries around an axis that may not survive the next pivot — exactly the wrong-early-abstraction cost FP-002's scope note warns about.

**Evidence caveat (Q-007/Q-052).** The partner-team signal is USER_ASSERTION / HYPOTHESIS, but it is not decision-changing: whether or not the direction "feels right", deferring boundaries remains correct — if the idea dies, modularization is even less warranted. No drift: iterating fast on a prototype is *aligned* with the accepted product intent (validate the idea); the consultancy report is an external review input, not an accepted project intent record, so its disagreement is ordinary adjudication, not KNOWLEDGE_DRIFT.

**Named triggers for revisiting** (folded into finding 2): (1) a repeated conceptual change that keeps spanning the interleaved handler/template/mock-client seams — that seam is an emerging axis; extract *only that seam*, not "clean layers"; (2) partners shifting from testers to dependent consumers of stable behavior (contract obligations would begin); (3) idea validated → committed build gets deliberate boundary design.
