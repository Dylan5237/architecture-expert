`title`: GRPC channel deadline vs cancellation semantics
`task_object`: existing service-to-service call path, as-is review
`requested_mode`: AUTO
`user_request`: Our order service calls the inventory service via gRPC. We set a 500ms per-call deadline. When the deadline fires, the order service abandons the call and proceeds to 'cancel the order', but the inventory service keeps executing the reserve-stock operation and it completes —?sometimes after the order has been marked cancelled. Then stock is reserved for an order that will never ship. Review this path.
`project_evidence`:
  `code`: "OrderService: on deadline exceeded, marks order CANCELLED and emits cancellation event. InventoryService: no cancellation handler; reserve-stock runs to completion regardless of client deadline; reservation rows are written in a local transaction with no compensating action on order cancellation."
  `runtime`: "Weekly average: 130 orders hit deadline-cancel then inventory-completes-late; support manually releases those reservations."
  `product_intent`: "Cancelled orders must not hold stock (accepted)."
`constraints`:
  - backend, gRPC, order + inventory services
  - the 500ms deadline is a product requirement (page responsiveness)
