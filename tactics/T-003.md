# T-003 Timeout / Deadline Bound

## Intent
Give per-work waits an effective time bound.

## Mechanism
Timeout = single-operation wait cap (V-008-A). Deadline = remaining end-to-end budget, possibly propagated (V-008-B, S-042). Missing one timeout is not automatically unbounded if another effective time bound exists.

## Applies When
External calls, locks, or waits that can stall a worker.

## Does Not Solve
Who cleans up after the timeout (T-002/MP-001). Overload admission (T-006). Cancellation is a different signal.

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-003 (wait/execution time dimension).

## Principle Links
MP-003.

## Trade-offs
Too-aggressive timeouts cause retry amplification (FP-011).

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
GC-004: indefinite lifetime with per-work time bounds.

## Evidence / Gaps
Platform-normative deadline propagation is scoped.
