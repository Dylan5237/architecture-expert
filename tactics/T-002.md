# T-002 Cancellation Propagation

## Intent
Stop in-flight work when its owner or caller no longer wants it, including cleanup of side effects where the model allows.

## Mechanism
Two non-alias senses (V-008-C): RPC/deadline propagation across calls (S-042); structured-scope cancellation of children (S-084..086).

## Applies When
Owned async/RPC work that can become orphaned if not cancelled.

## Does Not Solve
A timeout without an owner (deadline ≠ ownership, D-07). Kill without cleanup. Capacity bounds (T-003/T-004).

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-001 (orphan continuation). Stale completion from a false ordering assumption is FP-012.

## Principle Links
MP-001 owns cancellation/cleanup semantics.

## Trade-offs
Cooperative cancellation requires yield points; forced kill loses cleanup.

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
Supervised OTP processes remain owned even if they run indefinitely (GC-001).

## Evidence / Gaps
Dual semantics must stay distinct.
