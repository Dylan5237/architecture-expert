# T-014 Diagnostic Evidence Placement and Retention

## Intent
Place and retain the information needed for a concrete operational question at the path/boundary where it exists, or ensure it is derivable from what is retained.

## Mechanism
Question-relative capture: logs/metrics/traces/state snapshots as *means*, not a required stack. Proof can discharge only proved questions under satisfied assumptions (S-118).

## Applies When
Unanswered operational questions on running systems.

## Does Not Solve
Creating execution-model guarantees (MP-010). Privacy/security cost (trade-off; RQ5-003). “Install OpenTelemetry.”

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-007.

## Principle Links
MP-006.

## Trade-offs
Cost, privacy, and attack surface of retained evidence.

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
GC-008. Missing a vendor telemetry product is not a violation.

## Evidence / Gaps
RQ5-003. S-055 is not universal.
