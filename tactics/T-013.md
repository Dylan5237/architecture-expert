# T-013 Explicit Merge / Conflict Semantics

## Intent
Make conflict outcomes defined by declaring write/state authority and merge/consistency (V-010) rules.

## Mechanism
Single-writer, CRDT merge, append-only, or explicit last-write policy — chosen and declared, not implied. Local-first multi-writer is legal (S-107/S-119).

## Applies When
Concurrent writers, replicas, or caches.

## Does Not Solve
Lifecycle ownership of the writer process (MP-001). Execution-model ordering assumptions (MP-010). Event sourcing as a mandatory pattern.

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-009.

## Principle Links
MP-008. Not a single-writer principle.

## Trade-offs
Merge code and user-visible conflicts; simpler single-writer where it fits.

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
GC-013, GC-014.

## Evidence / Gaps
RQ5-005 isolation names vs actual semantics.
