# T-006 Admission Control

## Intent
Decide whether work may enter or continue before uncontrolled growth.

## Mechanism
A governed yes/no (or limited concurrency) at a chosen point on the path: service, gateway, or mesh.

## Applies When
Demand can exceed capacity.

## Does Not Solve
Propagating capacity upstream (T-007). Removing already-accepted work (T-008). Reducing function/quality (T-009). Priority scheduling is not admission (D-12).

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-010, FP-011.

## Principle Links
MP-009. Not required to be service-local.

## Trade-offs
Rejected work is user-visible; placement is an architectural choice.

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
GC-015 path-level admission.

## Evidence / Gaps
SRE/vendor concentrated; D-12 and RQ5-004 remain.
