# T-015 Idempotency under Explicit Delivery Semantics

## Intent
Make duplicate deliveries of the same operation equivalent to one effect, given an explicit at-least-once (or similar) delivery contract.

## Mechanism
Idempotency keys, exactly-once session protocols, or equivalent receiver-side dedup (V-021-B; S-038/S-043). Constitution: this is a mechanism/tactic/contract property (MF-1), not a principle.

## Applies When
Retries or at-least-once delivery exist.

## Does Not Solve
Retry storms (capacity still grows; T-005/T-006/T-008). Ownership of the consumer (MP-001). Retry ≠ idempotency.

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
Duplicate-effect damage that often accompanies FP-011; does not mitigate the storm itself.

## Principle Links
Supports safe use of T-005 under MP-003/MP-009. Not derived as an MP.

## Trade-offs
Storage of idempotency records; key design.

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
At-most-once paths may not need it. Do not treat “add retries” as this tactic.

## Evidence / Gaps
S-038/S-043. No extra delivery-semantics MP is created.
