# T-008 Rejection / Load Shedding

## Intent
Refuse or drop work so accepted load stays within capacity.

## Mechanism
Active rejection/removal, often by criticality (V-008-F). Direction is opposite to backpressure: drop/refuse at the point of control rather than signal upstream demand.

## Applies When
Overload where some work must be sacrificed to keep the rest viable.

## Does Not Solve
Admission-as-entry-decision (related but not identical: admission may queue with a cap; shedding removes). Upstream signaling (T-007). Keeping all functions at reduced quality (T-009).

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-010, FP-011.

## Principle Links
MP-009. Not required to live in service-local code.

## Trade-offs
User-visible loss; bad priority choice sheds the wrong work (still an implemented boundary).

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
GC-015: shedding/admission at gateway is compliant.

## Evidence / Gaps
SRE/AWS lineage; not universalized to embedded (D-12).
