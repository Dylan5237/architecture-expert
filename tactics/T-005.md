# T-005 Bounded Retry with Backoff/Jitter

## Intent
Re-attempt transient failures without removing the per-work retry bound or synchronized overload amplification.

## Mechanism
Retry = resend after failure; backoff = spacing; jitter = desynchronization (V-008-D). Infinite retry is unboundedness. Stacked retries are amplification.

## Applies When
Transient, independently retry-safe faults, with an explicit retry budget.

## Does Not Solve
At-least-once duplicate effects (T-015). Missing path-level capacity boundary (FP-010). Retry is not idempotency.

## Failure Patterns Mitigated
FP-003 (retry dimension); partial mitigation of FP-011 when bounded and jittered. Unbounded/synchronized retry *causes* FP-011.

## Principle Links
MP-003 for the retry bound; MP-009 for storm feedback.

## Trade-offs
Retries consume capacity that admission/shedding may need to protect.

## GOOD CASE / Misuse Boundary
GC-015: retries behind a governed path boundary.

## Evidence / Gaps
S-052 names the antipattern; does not forbid bounded retry.
