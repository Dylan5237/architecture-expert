# FP-007 Missing Distinguishing Evidence

## Failure Signature

For an operational question not discharged by a stronger proved guarantee, information needed to distinguish/attribute relevant runtime states was neither captured nor derivable from retained evidence.

## Causal Mechanism

Obtainability is decided by placement and retention on the path/boundary. Absent and underivable information cannot support reliable later attribution (MP-006).

## Preconditions / Trigger

A concrete unanswered operational question on a running/fault-response system.

## Amplification Path

Incident response guesses; repeated failure of the same undistinguished class.

## Observable Evidence

Post-incident inability to tell which of two states occurred; no retained signal at the relevant boundary.

## False Positives / GOOD CASES

- GC-008 seL4-class proof: questions actually proved under satisfied assumptions are discharged; others are not.
- Missing OpenTelemetry (or any stack) is not this FP.

## Consequences

Operability failure; unattributable incidents.

## Mitigation Tactics

T-014 Diagnostic Evidence Placement and Retention.

## Trade-offs / Scope

Question-relative (D-11). Privacy/cost are trade-offs, not the causal core. Vendor observability prose is not universal (S-055).

## Principle Links

MP-006. Excellent telemetry does not create MP-010 guarantees.

## Evidence / Gaps

RQ5-003 observation/privacy authority is weak; do not inflate it.
