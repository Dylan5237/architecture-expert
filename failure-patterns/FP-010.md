# FP-010 Ungoverned Overload Growth

## Failure Signature

Demand can exceed available capacity, yet the request/work path meets no effective governed capacity boundary before uncontrolled queue or resource growth.

## Causal Mechanism

Arrival rate > service rate without a boundary produces unstable backlog, then retry amplification and capacity-loss feedback (MP-009).

## Preconditions / Trigger

A path whose work can exceed capacity. Naturally bounded load is a NEEDS_EVIDENCE exemption (RQ5-004).

## Amplification Path

Queue growth → timeout → retry → further queue growth (FP-011 is the retry-specific family).

## Observable Evidence

Unbounded inbound queues, thread pools, or retained work with no admission/shedding/backpressure/degradation policy on the path.

## False Positives / GOOD CASES

- GC-015 gateway/path-level control without service-local shedding code.
- A fully bounded system that still drops the wrong traffic is a policy quality issue, not absence of MP-003 bounds (D-04).

## Consequences

Availability collapse under overload, including recovery traffic.

## Mitigation Tactics

T-006, T-007, T-008, T-009 as distinct choices, not aliases. Placement may be gateway/mesh/path.

## Trade-offs / Scope

SRE/vendor concentration. D-12: priority scheduling ≠ admission. Do not inherit MP-003's theoretical support.
