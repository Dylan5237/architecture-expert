# FP-006 Excess Privilege at Trust Boundary

## Failure Signature

On a boundary with untrusted input, multiple privilege levels, external integration, or agent action authority, a component holds more trust/permission than its duty requires.

## Causal Mechanism

Granted privilege determines the action set available after compromise or mistake (S-092). Excess privilege expands one dimension of blast potential; it is not a theorem bounding total damage.

## Preconditions / Trigger

Trust boundary (V-016-A) actually present.

## Amplification Path

Compromise or confused-deputy use of extra permissions reaches data/actions outside the duty.

## Observable Evidence

Agent/tool credentials that can perform unrelated destructive actions; over-broad roles crossing a trust boundary.

## False Positives / GOOD CASES

- GC-012 closed/coarse-grained local trust without relevant untrusted/cross-privilege/agent-action boundary.

## Consequences

Security and reliability loss along the privilege dimension.

## Mitigation Tactics

T-011 Least-Privilege Restriction. Capability tokens (V-016-C) are a scoped mechanism; RQ3-004 open.

## Trade-offs / Scope

Do not universalize enterprise zero trust. Do not treat this FP as MP-004 cascade (D-05).
