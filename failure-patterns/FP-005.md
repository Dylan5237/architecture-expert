# FP-005 Coupled-Defense Amplification

## Failure Signature

A defensive path (fallback, protection control-plane, retry-into-shared-pool) shares fate with the primary failure and enlarges blast radius instead of containing it.

## Causal Mechanism

If the defense is coupled to the same dependency/control plane as the fault, the protection mechanism becomes an amplifier (S-050 fallback warning; S-063 BGP/DNS protection coupling).

## Preconditions / Trigger

Fallback or protection that uses the same scarce resource, identity, or control plane as the failing primary.

## Amplification Path

Primary fails → defense activates → defense also saturates or disables remaining capacity.

## Observable Evidence

Both primary and fallback overloaded together; protection action removes more service than the original fault.

## False Positives / GOOD CASES

A scoped fallback that does not share the failing bottleneck is not this FP. Absence of fallback is not automatically a violation of MP-004.

## Consequences

Designed containment is inverted.

## Mitigation Tactics

T-010 with explicit decoupling of defense from the protected resource. Degradation (T-009) is a different mechanism and must not be treated as alias of isolation.

## Trade-offs / Scope

AWS/SRE + one incident family. Do not generalize every missing circuit breaker into this FP (S-069 is historical-background only).
